// the honest bit

Agents, not automations

An automation follows a rule. An agent reads the situation, decides, and hands you the part that needs a human. That difference is the whole product. It is also why most of this work fails, in three predictable ways.

fails

It works in the demo

Easy cases get automated. The odd ones break it. Your team goes back to doing it by hand.

so: we build the awkward cases first. If they can't be handled, we tell you it isn't worth automating.
fails

The builder leaves

One person understood it. They finished their contract. Now nobody will touch it or switch it off.

so: it runs in your accounts, documented, with your team trained. You own it outright.
fails

People stop using it

Usage drops quietly. Nobody checks. It gets cancelled a year later, still being paid for.

so: we check usage at month one and month three. Unused means we fix it or remove it.

// straight answers

Before you ask

Our data is a mess. Are we too disorganised?

Everyone's is. Shared drives, three spreadsheets that disagree, a CRM half the team ignores. That's the normal starting point. If something needs tidying before it's worth automating, we'll tell you in the map.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything involving money or a customer needs a person to approve it. Everything else logs what it did, so mistakes are visible and reversible. Any workflow can be switched off on its own.

What happens when you leave?

You own it. It runs in your accounts, it's documented, and your team is trained on it. Nothing of ours sits in the middle. If we vanished tomorrow it would keep running.

Isn't this just ChatGPT?

ChatGPT waits for you to ask. An agent runs on its own, against your real data, and knows when to stop and get you. The models are cheap and you already have them. What you're paying for is knowing which jobs to point them at, handling the cases that break them, and it still working in six months.

How much does it cost?

Each build is priced against the hours it replaces, and quoted before you commit. If the numbers don't work, we'll say so rather than sell you a retainer.

Do we have to move onto new software?

No. Agents run inside your CRM, your sheets, your inbox and your project tool. Nothing gets ripped out and there are no new logins for your team.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Send an email address and the map comes back in writing. Talk to us afterwards only if it's worth talking about.
